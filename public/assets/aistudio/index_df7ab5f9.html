About Focusaur

The Science of Cognitive Focus Conditioning

Focusaur is a research-grounded cognitive training platform crafted to measure and improve human attentional control, inhibitory functions, working memory capacity, and mental processing speed.

Inspired by seminal neuroscience paradigms including the Stroop Effect (1935), Schulte Grids (used by cosmonauts and speed-readers), and the Continuous Performance Task (CPT), each interactive test exercises targeted neural pathways in the prefrontal cortex and parietal networks.

Regular short training sessions (5 to 10 minutes daily) challenge neuroplasticity, strengthen mental stamina, and reduce susceptibility to digital attention fragmentation.
